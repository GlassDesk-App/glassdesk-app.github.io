GlassDesk

Frosted-glass desktop widgets for Windows 11.

GlassDesk puts small, see-through widgets on your desktop: clocks, weather, system monitor, notes, reminders, news feeds, markets, music controls, calendars and more. Each widget sits on your wallpaper with a real frosted-glass background, follows your chosen accent colour, and uses almost no computer power when idle.

Calendar and tasks

If you choose to, you can sign in with Google or Microsoft inside GlassDesk so your calendar widgets show your own events and your to-do widget shows your open tasks. GlassDesk only asks to read them. It cannot create, change or delete anything in your account.

Your data stays on your PC

GlassDesk has no servers and no account system of its own. Sign-ins are kept in Windows Credential Manager on your own computer, and your calendar and task information is fetched directly from Google or Microsoft to your PC and shown in your widgets. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Read the full privacy policy.
